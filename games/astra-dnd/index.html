    THE HOLLOW MARCHES / CHAPTER ONEAn original adventure ·

    At this table

    A solo, authored adventure using a streamlined fifth-edition ruleset. The narrator follows branching scenes and commands; it is not a live AI dungeon master.

    • Checks: roll a d20 + the displayed modifier against a Difficulty Class (DC). Failed checks change your route; they don’t stop the story.
    • Combat: initiative decides who strikes first. Each turn, take one action. Attack rolls must meet Armour Class (AC); a natural 20 doubles damage dice and a natural 1 misses.
    • Advantage rolls two d20s and keeps the higher. Rogue hiding and several story choices can grant it.
    • Potions take an action in combat. One short rest restores hit points outside combat. At 0 HP, this solo adventure ends; you can begin again.
    • These are custom level-2 presets, with simplified class features, actions, resting and defeat. Not a complete 5e rules simulator.
