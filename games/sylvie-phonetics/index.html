How to play

  • Tap the game you want to try. Sound practice comes first, then word building and a tiny reader.
  • Use the microphone round only when Chrome shows it is ready; otherwise tap the letters.
  • Look for the picture and sound clues together. Keep the pace gentle and celebrate every success.

Parent coach

The app teaches through play. This screen keeps the explicit teaching sequence clear.

Research sequenceStart with rhyme and syllable play, then first/middle/last sounds, then sound boxes and blending, then letter links and tiny decodable reading.
Scaffold missesHint first, model second, retry once, then move on gently. The goal is sound investigation, not testing.
Best teaching orderOral sound play first, then letter-sound links, then CVC blending and spelling, then tiny decodable reading, and only later digraphs and trickier patterns.
Gifted-child adjustmentMove faster after real mastery, but do not increase emotional load. Stretch with patterns, invention, and noticing, not with heaps of harder words.
Clean sound languageSay “This letter can show /m/” rather than “M says muh”. Keep phonemes clipped, especially for stop sounds.
When to stopStop after success. Five happy minutes on May 3, 2026 beats thirty overloaded minutes on any day.
How to explain hard and soft G

Use it as a noticing game, not a mastery target. Say: “Most of the time g says /g/ like goat and grape. Sometimes it says /j/ like gem and giraffe. English has patterns and some cheeky exceptions.”

What is already built into the game

First sounds, letter-sound matching, middle and last sound listening, tracing, memory matching, syllable clapping, vowel listening, word building, oral blending, rhyming, digraph noticing, spoken spelling with the microphone, sound boxes for phoneme counting, repeating patterns & sorting (early maths language), review rounds for missed words, tiny decodable reading, story building, environmental print and pattern detective games.

Early Years Learning Framework (EYLF) note

This app aligns with fragments of Australia's national Early Years Learning Framework through play-based literacy rehearsal and intentional adult coaching—it does not replace preschool, kindergarten / Pre-Prep, or educator assessment. Sharing the session recap with your early childhood educator is optional and can support continuity.

Speech output limits

All speech uses your device's browser synthesis (offline). Pace and clarity vary by operating system—use the speech rate tweak and Robot voice sparingly alongside your own modelling.

Browser notes for laptop and Android

Speech playback should work in Chrome on both devices after a tap. Microphone spelling uses the Web Speech API. Chrome support varies by device, so there is always a non-microphone fallback on screen.
