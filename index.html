How to play

Desktop: WASD or arrows move · drag to look, or select Capture mouse (L) to look around freely without holding a button. Esc releases the mouse; double-click also captures it · Shift runs · C crouches · Space jumps · E uses an exhibit · M toggles the bird's-eye map · T jumps the clock · Esc closes panels.

Adventure: Press J or open the Quest Journal. Find twelve glowing relics outside public houses, solve the puzzles and return to the Guild Chest near the start.

Town life: the town runs on its own clock (a day lasts 72 minutes; the Town Guide kiosk near the start can speed it up). Farms on street 8 grow food, trucks deliver it to the mill, bakery and shops, and the villagers work, shop, cook, eat and sleep. Stop anyone for a chat — ask what they ate, for gossip, or for a relic hint. Shop counters, farms and the Town Hall ledger open with E.

Phone: left thumb drags the joystick · right thumb drags to look · ⤒ jumps · ✋ uses · 🏃 and 🧎 toggle run and crouch.

Walk up to a front door and it opens; keep walking to go inside. Walk back out through the door you came in by. Your position saves automatically.

NPCs: Approach a character and press E or ✋, or tap 💬 NPCs. Characters and villagers chat about anything through the town's AI service (no key needed); your messages are sent to it, and they answer offline when it is unavailable.
