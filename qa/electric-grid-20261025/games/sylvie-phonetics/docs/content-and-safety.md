# Sylvie Phonics Adventure Content and Safety

## Audience

The app supports preschool/early-years phonics practice with parent or educator co-play. It is not a formal assessment product.

## Safety Commitments

- Local-only progress storage.
- No child account.
- No server upload of session data.
- Adult-facing curriculum language stays out of the core child play path where possible.
- Optional content files are validated before use.
- Child-facing play remains gentle, short, and low-pressure.

## Speech and Microphone Notes

Speech uses browser/device speech synthesis, so voice quality, availability, pronunciation, and timing vary. Some browsers may not support every speech or microphone feature.

Robot voice is a blending support. It should be paired with adult modelling rather than treated as a perfect pronunciation source.

## Curriculum Framing

The EYLF mapping is intended to help families and educators understand design intent. It does not certify NQF, EYLF, or school-readiness compliance.

## Cultural Safety

Optional content bundles must be reviewed by humans before publication. Content that references Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander stories, language, places, images, naming, or community practices requires appropriate family, educator, and community guidance.

## Optional Content Bundle

The app can load:

```text
content/bundle.json
```

If the file is missing or invalid, the bundled content remains unchanged. See [Optional Content Bundle Schema](content-bundle-schema.md).
