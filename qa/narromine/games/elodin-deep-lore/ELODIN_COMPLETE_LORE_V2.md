## I. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION & PRESENCE

### From NOTW (Admissions, Ch. 9)

> *"Elodin was younger than the others by at least a dozen years. Clean shaven with deep eyes. Medium height, medium build, there was nothing particularly striking about him, except for the way he sat at the table, one moment watching something intently, the next minute bored and letting his attention wander among the high beams of the ceiling above. He was almost like a child who had been forced to sit down with adults."*

His voice has a peculiar quality:

> *"He had an accent that I couldn't quite place. Or perhaps it was that his voice held a certain resonance. When he spoke, everyone at the desk stirred slightly, then grew still, like leaves touched by the wind."*

### Behavioral Markers

- Often barefoot or in stocking feet
- Wears master's robes "haphazardly" or not at all
- Moves with unusual physical ease (slides on marble floors, leaps for leaves, childlike motion)
- Eyes are described as "deep," "sharp," "alert," and capable of looking "into" rather than merely "at" a person
- His light voice "moves through the deep places in your chest"

### Critical Quote: His Own History

From NOTW Ch. 46, Elodin explains his escape:

> *"You see, once upon a time Elodin the Great found himself locked in a high tower. He had been stripped of his tools: his coin, key, and candle. Furthermore, his cell had no door worth mentioning. No window that could be breached. Even the name of the wind was hidden from him by the clever machinations of his captors."*

Then: 

> *"But Elodin the Great knew the names of all things, and so all things were his to command." He faced the grey wall beside the windows. "He said to the stone: BREAK! and the..."*

He then speaks **CYAERBASALIEN** and the stone wall dissolves.

Crucially:

> *"Sod me, they changed it," he said quietly to himself. "Huh." He stepped closer to the wall and lay a hand on it.*

**What this tells us:** The warden knew a Name and changed it between the first escape and Kvothe's visit.

> *"Two years. Able to see this balcony but not stand on it. Able to see the wind, but not hear it, not feel it on my face."*

## III. TEACHING PHILOSOPHY & THE SLEEPING MIND

### The Core Doctrine: From NOTW Ch. 46 (Elodin to Kvothe)

> *"Each of us has two minds: a waking mind and a sleeping mind. Our waking mind is what thinks and talks and reasons. But the sleeping mind is more powerful. It sees deeply to the heart of things. It is the part of us that dreams. It remembers everything. It gives us intuition."*

The pivotal revelation:

> *"Your waking mind does not understand the nature of names. Your sleeping mind does. It already knows many things that your waking mind does not."*

### How Names Work: From NOTW Ch. 46 (Re'lar explanation)

> *"Names are the shape of the world, and a man who can speak them is on the road to power."*

The crucial distinction—words vs. Names:

> *"Words can light fires in the minds of men. Words can wring tears from the hardest hearts. There are seven words that will make a person love you. There are ten words that will break a strong man's will. But a word is nothing but a painting of a fire. A name is the fire itself."*

### Teaching Methods: Extreme Pedagogy

From NOTW:
- The pinecone test (arbitrary task; real test is something else)
- Hours of riddling
- Making Kvothe drink applejack then read Teccam's Theophany
- Three-day blindfold
- Staying awake for nearly five days on coffee

From WMF Ch. 72 (Archive rooftop scene):

> *"There was a great beast of a thunderstorm rolling in, and Elodin decided it would do me good to spend some time in the middle of it... Elodin simply stole the key... Elodin insisted that we strip ourselves naked, wrap our clothes in an oilskin, and weigh them down with a brick."*

The clothes blow away in the wind. The key blows away. They're found naked and wet on the roof by Lorren and the scrivs.

> *"Elodin laughed his head off at the whole thing."*

But the point was real:

> *"Elodin went to great lengths to wake my sleeping mind. Ridiculous lengths, really. And much to my surprise, our work paid dividends. I called the name of the wind three times that term."*

## IV. WHAT ELODIN KNOWS

### He Knows About the Faen World

From WMF Ch. 71 (Shaed scene):

> *"What's this?" he exclaimed when we crossed paths in a small courtyard outside Mains. "How did you come to be enshaedn? Your cloak, boy. Your turning cape. How in God's sweet grace did you tumble onto a shaed?"*

He knows it intimately:

> *"I wouldn't be much of a namer if I couldn't spot a faerie cloak a dozen feet away... Oh, that's just lovely. Here's a piece of old magic man rarely lays a finger on."*

When Kvothe tells the story of Felurian and Fae:

> *"Elodin proved to be a surprisingly attentive audience and was especially interested in the fight Felurian and I had had when she had tried to bend me to her will."*

He peppers Kvothe with questions about calling the wind, and his response:

> *"It's a good sign when a student goes chasing the wind and catches it. That's twice you've called it now. It can only get easier."*

When Kvothe mentions he called it three times:

> *"He laughed. 'You chased it to the edge of the map!' he said, making a broad motion with his splayed left hand. Stunned, I realized it was Adem hand-talk for amazed respect."*

**He knows Adem hand-talk.** This is not incidental.

### He Knows About Naming and Identity

From WMF Ch. 91 (Denna's name-changing):

When Kvothe asks what he thinks of someone who keeps changing their name, Elodin's first reaction is panic:

> *"What? He sat up suddenly, his eyes wild and panicked. 'What have you done?'"*

His relief when it's not Fela:

> *"Elodin's face grew ashen. 'Fela?' he said. 'Oh no. No. She wouldn't do something like that. She's too smart for that.'"*

When he learns it's Denna:

> *"'Oh,' Elodin said, relaxing. He leaned back against the tree, laughing softly. 'Calling names,' he said with tangible relief. 'God's bones, boy, I thought—' He broke off, shaking his head."*

His analysis:

> *"It could indicate she doesn't know who she is, or that she does know, and doesn't like it. It could indicate restlessness and dissatisfaction. It could mean her nature is changeable and she shifts her name..."*

**Why was he panicked about Fela changing her name?** This is crucial and unanswered.

### He Knows the Four-Plate Door

From NOTW Ch. 46, when Kvothe asks about the door in the Archives:

> *"'Oh no. No no. You don't aim for small secrets do you?' He clapped me on the back as if I'd just made an especially good joke. 'Valaritas. God. I can still remember what it was like, standing down there looking at the door, wondering.'"*

Then the crucial line:

> *"He laughed again. 'Merciful Tehlu, it almost killed me.'"*

**The four-plate door almost killed Elodin.** This is cannon and crucial.

## V. HIS RELATIONSHIP WITH KVOTHE

### The Initial Assessment

First time at Admissions (NOTW Ch. 9):

> *"He lifted a hand, his index finger pointing upward. 'How many fingers am I holding up?'... 'At least one,' I said. 'Probably no more than six.'"*

Elodin's reaction:

> *"He broke into a broad smile... He lowered his hands to the table in front of him, and grew suddenly serious. 'Do you know the seven words that will make a woman love you?' ...  'They exist.' He reassured me, and sat back with a look of contentment."*

### The Testing Phase

The pinecone test and the walk (NOTW Ch. 46):

> *"Elodin stopped walking abruptly and turned to face me. 'Fine,' he said. 'Go find me three pinecones.' ... 'Fine. I'll show you why.'"*

The three-question limit.

The rooftop test:

> *"'Jump,' he said. 'Jump off this roof.'"*

When Kvothe jumps and hits hard:

> *"'Congratulations,' he said. 'That was the stupidest thing I've ever seen.' His expression was a mix of awe and disbelief. 'Ever.'"*

But then:

> *"Before helping me limp to the Medica, Elodin made it clear that anyone stupid enough to jump off a roof was too reckless to be allowed to hold a spoon in his presence, let alone study something as 'profound and volatile' as naming."*

Yet immediately after:

> *"Elodin spoke, seeming to startle everyone at the table. 'I can think of students currently enrolled in the Arcanum who would be hard pressed to complete a double binding, let alone draw enough heat to blister a man's foot to the knee.' ... He smiled happily at me again."*

**His intervention saves Kvothe from expulsion.**

### The Sponsorship & Re'lar Elevation

From NOTW Ch. 46:

> *"'I move that Kvothe be raised to the rank of Re'lar.' ... 'All in favor?' All hands save Hemme's were raised in a single motion."*

He becomes Kvothe's sponsor and explains the Arcanum:

> *"'The Arcanum was a very small, very secret thing. They had a ranking system among themselves... One entered this group by proving they could see things for what they really were. They became E'lir, which means see-er... By speaking.' ... 'Names are the shape of the world, and a man who can speak them is on the road to power. Back in the beginning, the Arcanum was a small collection of men who understood things. Men who knew powerful names... In those days the Arcanum was a strong brandy. Now it is well-watered wine.'"*

### The Disciplining & The Calm

Critical moment in NOTW Ch. 51, after Kvothe calls the wind in anger at Ambrose:

> *"Elodin entered the room without knocking or announcing himself... 'Kvothe?' Elodin repeated. When I didn't respond or look up from my lute he reached forward and gently tipped my chin up until I met his eye. 'Aerlevsedi,' he said. 'Say it.'"*

Kvothe repeats the Name (say

ing it numbly), and then:

> *"He leaned forward and his lips brushed my ear. I felt his breath. He spoke... and the storm stilled. I found a place to land."*

The effect is described as like a spinning child suddenly stopping:

> *"Then you stop and the world lurches into regular shape. The dizziness strikes you like a thunderclap... That is what happened when Elodin stilled the storm in my head."*

**Elodin knows Kvothe's true name and can use it to calm him at a deep level.**

## VI. AURI & THE ROOKERY CONNECTION

From NOTW Ch. 53, Auri speaks of seeing Elodin:

> *"'I saw Elodin.' ... 'Was he on top of things, too?' She nodded again, chewing. 'Did he see you?' Her smile burst out again making her look closer to eight than eighteen. 'Nobody sees me. Besides, he was busy listening to the wind.' She cupped her hands around her mouth and made a hooting noise."*

From WMF Ch. 66 (rooftop encounter):

> *"Before I could respond, Elodin stepped around a large brick chimney as casually as if he were out for an afternoon stroll... 'Master Elodin,' I said in my gentlest, friendliest tones, desperately hoping he wouldn't do anything that might startle Auri into running."*

Elodin's interaction with Auri:

> *"'And what brings the two of you out this fine night?' Elodin asked... 'Kvothe has brought me lovely things,' she said. 'He brought me bee beer and barley bread and a smoked fish with a harp where its heart should be.' 'Ah,' Elodin said, stepping away from the chimney. He patted his robes until he found something in a pocket. He held it out to her. 'I'm afraid I've only brought you a cinnas fruit.'"*

He makes a *formal bow* to Auri. They clearly know each other, and he is respectful.
