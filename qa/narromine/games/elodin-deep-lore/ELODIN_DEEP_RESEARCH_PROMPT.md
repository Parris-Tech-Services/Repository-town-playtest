# ELODIN DEEP RESEARCH PROMPT
### For use with Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or any capable AI research assistant

---

## PURPOSE

This prompt is designed to push beyond the known canon and generate the most complete picture of Elodin possible — including conjecture, extrapolation, and structured speculation about his past, present, and future role in the Kingkiller Chronicle. It is intended to be run against an AI with full knowledge of the series and any available web access for fan theory sources.

---

## THE PROMPT

---

You are the most thorough Kingkiller Chronicle scholar in existence. Your task is to produce the definitive deep analysis of **Elodin** — Master Namer, former Chancellor of the University, escapee of the Rookery, and arguably the most powerful and most deliberately obscure figure in Patrick Rothfuss's series.

I want you to move through four layers of analysis:

---

### LAYER 1: CANON INVENTORY

Compile every single fact that is **directly stated or unambiguously shown** about Elodin across all canon sources:
- *The Name of the Wind* (2007)
- *The Wise Man's Fear* (2011)
- *The Slow Regard of Silent Things* (2014)
- Any Rothfuss-confirmed statements (interviews, conventions, blog posts)

For each fact, note:
- Where it appears (book, approximate chapter/scene)
- Whether it is stated directly, implied, or shown through behavior
- Whether any other character has confirmed or contradicted it

Include: physical description, timeline, abilities, relationships, all scene appearances, all Named words spoken, all votes cast at faculty hearings, all interactions with Auri, Whin, Kvothe, Kilvin, and other Masters.

---

### LAYER 2: THE GAP ANALYSIS

Identify every significant **gap or ambiguity** in what we know about Elodin. For each gap:
1. State precisely what we don't know
2. List every textual clue that is relevant to filling that gap
3. Rate how central this gap is to the series' major mysteries (1-5)

Key gaps to address:
- What exactly happened before the Rookery? What did he do / discover / attempt?
- What caused him to break — or appear to break?
- Why did his captors suppress the Name of the Wind specifically? What does that imply?
- What is the full nature of his relationship with Auri? Is she a former student? Did he cause her breakdown, or try to prevent it?
- What does he know about the Chandrian that he isn't saying?
- What does he know about the Amyr?
- What does he know about Kvothe's heritage (Lackless bloodline)?
- What is "Unlikely Maths" — is this a real discipline? A joke? A cover?
- Why does he come and go from Haven to visit Alder Whin? What is Whin's significance?
- What happened to Kvothe's ability to Name the wind after the head injury? Is this foreshadowing permanent loss?

---

### LAYER 3: STRUCTURED CONJECTURE

Now step beyond the text. Using everything you know — canon, fan theory, Rothfuss interviews, narrative structure — make your best educated guesses about the following. For each conjecture, rate your confidence (Low / Medium / High) and explain your reasoning:

**A. His Past**

1. What is Elodin's origin? Where did he come from before the University?
2. Who taught him? Or did he develop his Naming ability independently?
3. What specifically sent him to the Rookery? (Select the most likely theory from: deep Naming, attempted Shaping, external encounter, political enemies, discovering forbidden knowledge)
4. What was he like as a student? As a young Chancellor?
5. Is it possible he is older than he appears — that Naming or Shaping has extended his life?
6. Did he ever encounter the Chandrian directly? If so, what happened, and how did he survive?
7. Did he ever speak with the Cthaeh? What would that mean for his subsequent behavior?

**B. His Present Knowledge**

8. Does Elodin know Kvothe's true Name? If so, has he ever spoken it?
9. Does Elodin know who Kvothe's mother really was (Netalia Lackless)? What does that mean for how he treats Kvothe?
10. Does Elodin know that the Chandrian specifically killed Kvothe's parents because of Arliden's song?
11. What does Elodin know about the Doors of Stone?
12. Is there a connection between the copper-warding of his cell (which blocked the Name of the Wind) and the Doors of Stone? Could copper be the material of the Doors?
13. Does Elodin know the Name of the Moon? If so, does that give him a connection to Felurian's world?
14. Is Elodin an Amyr — active, former, or affiliated?
15. What is "the greater danger" that Elodin implicitly protects people from by withholding knowledge?

**C. His Future (Doors of Stone)**

16. What role will Elodin play in Book 3? Based on the narrative trajectory, what does he need to do?
17. Will he reveal what he knows about Kvothe's parentage?
18. Will he teach Kvothe something that only Elodin could teach — possibly related to Shaping?
19. Is it possible Elodin dies in Book 3? What would his death mean thematically?
20. If Kvothe is responsible for "bringing down the moon" or opening/closing the Doors of Stone, how does Elodin's teaching make that possible?
21. Will the relationship between Elodin and Auri become explicit — will Kvothe understand what they share?
22. Kvothe loses his Naming ability at the end of Book 2 (the blow to the head). Will Elodin be the one to help him recover it, or will that recovery be Kvothe's alone?

---

### LAYER 4: THE COMPLETE ELODIN PORTRAIT

Synthesize everything into a single comprehensive portrait of Elodin: who he was, who he is, and who he is likely to become.

This portrait should include:
- A psychological profile: what drives him, what he fears, what he loves, how he thinks
- A magical profile: what he can do, what he refuses to do, what he is still learning
- A narrative profile: what story role he serves, what the series needs him to do, what his arc is
- A relational profile: how he sees each person he has meaningful connection to (Kvothe, Auri, Whin, Kilvin, and any others)
- A mystery profile: the three most important things about him that the series has not yet revealed, and your best guess at each

Conclude with:

**If you had to bet your life on one theory about Elodin's deepest secret — the thing he knows that no one else alive knows and that will matter most in Book 3 — what would it be? Make your case.**

## SUPPLEMENTARY QUESTIONS (run separately or as follow-ups)

After the main prompt, these targeted questions go even deeper:

1. **The Stone Name**: CYAERBASALIEN dissolves stone. What does this Name tell us about how Naming works — specifically, is it a command, a recognition, or a form of Address? Compare to how Kvothe calls Aerlevsedi (the wind). What is the grammar of Names in this world?

2. **The Copper Question**: Copper blocks sympathy. It was used to suppress Elodin's Naming. Does copper block *all* Naming, or just specific Names? What does it mean that they suppressed Aerlevsedi but not CYAERBASALIEN? Did they not know he had that Name — or can copper only block a finite set of Names?

3. **The Seven Words**: Elodin says there are seven words that will make a woman love you, and they exist. What are they? This has never been answered — speculate freely. Are they a Name? A Shaping? Something else? What does it reveal about Elodin that he knows this?

4. **The Giller Connection**: Alder Whin was Elodin's giller. Whin is now in Haven, broken. What happened to Whin? Was Whin present for whatever sent Elodin to the Rookery? Did Whin break first, or after? Is the broken giller a warning about what Kvothe risks in studying with Elodin?

5. **Unlikely Maths**: What is this class actually? The name is a joke — but is there a real discipline hiding behind it? Could "unlikely maths" be the mathematics of probability manipulation, of synchronicity, of Names' relationships to each other? Is this how Elodin teaches without appearing to teach?

6. **Elodin and Felurian**: Felurian is ancient, predates the creation of Faen, and knows things about the war between the Singers and the Shapers. Does Elodin know Felurian? Has he been to the Fae realm? His knowledge of Shaping's consequences suggests awareness of that ancient war. What connection, if any, exists between them?

7. **The Name of the Wind and the Doors of Stone**: The Lackless rhyme says something sleeps behind a door with no hinges, no bolt, no key. Wind passes through all such gaps. If Kvothe learns to use Aerlevsedi with complete mastery, can he pass through the Doors of Stone? Is this why Elodin teaches him this Name specifically?

8. **Elodin's Own Name**: If someone knew Elodin's true Name, what power would they have over him? Has anyone ever tried? Does he protect himself by changing how he presents — by never being fully knowable — the way a Named thing can be made unknowable by altering its fundamental nature?
