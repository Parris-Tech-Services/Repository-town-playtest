    How to play

    Desktop: WASD or arrows move · drag to look, or select Capture mouse (L) to look around freely without holding a button. Esc releases the mouse; double-click also captures it · Shift runs · C crouches · Space jumps · E uses an exhibit · M toggles the bird's-eye map · T jumps the clock · Esc closes panels.

    Adventure: Press J or open the Quest Journal. Find twelve glowing relics outside public houses, solve the puzzles and return to the Guild Chest near the start.

    In-game emergencies: Tap 🚨 000 (Game) to simulate a Police, Fire or Ambulance emergency. This never contacts real emergency services. For a real emergency, use your phone to call 000.

    Finding open shops: choose 🕒 Shop hours to see daily hours, live entry status and directions for all shops and services.

    Items: Find the torch beside the Town Guide. Press E or tap ✋ to pick it up. Open your Bag to hold, put away or drop it on clear ground. Dropped equipment stays there for you to collect, including after a reload.

    Town life: the town runs on its own clock (a day lasts 72 minutes; the Town Guide kiosk near the start can speed it up). Farms on street 8 grow food, trucks deliver it to the mill, bakery and shops, and the villagers work, shop, cook, eat and sleep. Stop anyone for a chat — ask what they ate, for gossip, or for a relic hint. Shop counters, farms and the Town Hall ledger open with E.

    Flying at Narromine Aerodrome (YNRM): Find Narromine Aerodrome using the search, walk to either parked aircraft and press E or ✋. W/S changes throttle, A/D steer or bank, Arrow ↑/↓ pitch, V switches cockpit/chase views and R lines up on runway 11 while stopped. On phones, use the flight instrument panel’s hold-buttons. E or 🚶 Exit works only once landed and stopped. Runway positions and flying characteristics are simplified game interpretations, not real navigation guidance.

    Driving: The instrument dashboard shows live km/h, fuel, estimated range, engine coolant temperature, gear and odometer. Tap Radio ON to hear original in-game stations; use the arrows to tune and the volume slider to adjust. The dashboard closes when you get out. The coolant and RPM readings are simplified game simulations, not real vehicle diagnostics.

    Movement speed: Open Settings to adjust walking and running from 0.1× to 5×, then use Reset to normal when needed. Your preference saves on this device.

    Sun and shadows: The outdoor sun follows Narromine's actual seasonal sky path based on the in-game date and NSW time. Change the moving building/tree shadows in Settings → Sun and shadows. High quality uses more battery; automatic is safest on phones.

    Compass: Toggle the small top-right N/E/S/W heading compass in Settings → Navigation. The fixed top pointer is where your camera is facing; the dial points to map north. Your preference saves on this device.

    Phone: left thumb drags the joystick · right thumb drags to look · ⤒ jumps · ✋ uses · 🏃 and 🧎 toggle run and crouch.

    Walk up to a front door and it opens; keep walking to go inside. Walk back out through the door you came in by. Your position saves automatically.

    NPC conversations on phones: Expand the town controls with +, then choose Dialogue settings to turn nearby conversation pop-ups on or off. Choose ⚡ Grid or Ask AI from that same + menu; neither stays over the game view. Tap ✕ on a pop-up to dismiss that yarn. Yarns, NPCs and Ask AI remain available whenever you choose them.

    NPCs: Approach a character and press E or ✋, or tap 💬 NPCs. Characters and villagers chat about anything through the town's AI service (no key needed); your messages are sent to it, and they answer offline when it is unavailable.
