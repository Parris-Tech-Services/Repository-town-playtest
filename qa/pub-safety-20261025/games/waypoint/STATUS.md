# Status — Waypoint

## What works now
- Five-step decision flow with progress bar, back/next, and validation.
- Tagging (work/family/health/money/project/faith).
- Saved decision cards in localStorage (last 100).
- Export to JSON and Markdown.
- Empty state before first decision.
